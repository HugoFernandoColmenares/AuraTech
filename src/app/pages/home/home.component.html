Dashboard

AuraTech is a sales intelligence workspace: map any Excel workbook to sales fields, review KPIs and charts, then persist cleaned records. It is built for merchandisers, operations analysts, and independent operators who need a lightweight reporting loop without a full data warehouse.

The demo does not assume a fixed spreadsheet layout. Upload your file, map columns with Custom Excel, review session rows, and export to the database when you are ready.

Session vs database

Uploaded files are parsed in the browser and flagged as session data. They are not persisted until you use Export to DB. The toolbar icon shows how many rows are still in session versus already stored.
