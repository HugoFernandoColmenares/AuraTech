Custom Excel (Sales Report)

When your spreadsheet does not match the generic template, choose Custom Excel on upload. A guided modal lets you pick the worksheet, map each sales column visually, preview the result, and optionally save the mapping as a reusable template.

Session vs database

Uploaded files are parsed in the browser and flagged as session data. They are not persisted until you use Export to DB. The toolbar icon shows how many rows are still in session versus already stored in Supabase.
